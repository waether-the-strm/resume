## What went wrong, and what it taught

- **A flaky test was a real defect.** "Passes when run alone" hid a page that could be left stuck
  between stops. The subagent was told to find the cause before touching the test, and to report a
  cause in the page instead of hiding it; that instruction is what found it.
- **A decorative mark failed the contrast scan, rightly.** Text is text to a scanner. The mark is
  now painted, not written, which is also the honest markup for decoration. Low contrast by choice
  has to be said in the structure, not excused in a test.
- **A fixed height copied from the prototype dimmed the Polish page.** The band under the guide was
  136 px on a phone because the prototype's was; in Polish the guide is three lines and the scene
  reaches up behind it. Sized by the guide it is right in every language. The prototype's numbers
  were tuned for one language.
- **The Polish look does not fit a phone in the tour.** Nobody had looked at Polish on a phone with
  the guide and the blob present. Measured and written down (#12); not fixed, because the fix is
  copy or type, and both are Jan's.
- **Finish is seen on the render, not in the diff.** #117 passed every check with its title row
  pushed into the corner and a rule that stopped half-way. A task about how something looks is
  reviewed by looking at every state, and the hand-over should say what was looked at.
- **The lead session cut a file in half** (`i18n/content.ts`) by replacing "from here to the end"
  where a function ended earlier. The type check caught it at once. Replace a block by both of its
  ends.
- **Again the verdict was posted before the merge returned** (#117). It merged, but the order is
  wrong; the note from yesterday did not change the habit, so it is now a step: merge, read the
  state, then comment.

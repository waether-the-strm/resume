C. Snapping to keyframes

Dashed lines are the keyframes. Try a slow drag and a flick in each mode (wheel, trackpad or touch). Readout shows scroll position in screens.

Mode
Why proximity, never mandatory. The stage is driven by continuous progress; the in-between values are the content. mandatory forces every gesture to land on a snap point, so the reader can never rest between keyframes, and on a sticky track the browser may pick far-away points after a flick. proximity only snaps when scrolling stops near a target, and the user can always stop elsewhere. Snap targets are 1px hidden elements inside the track at the keyframe offsets (scroll-snap-align:start); the sticky stage itself is not a target.
.scroller{scroll-snap-type:y proximity}
.snap{position:absolute;top:calc(var(--kf)/5.6*100%);height:1px;scroll-snap-align:start}
